## 创作提示词

生成方式：内置 image_gen；三款分别独立生成，未使用外部参考图。

### 方案 1

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished landscape brand presentation for ActionVault, a native macOS application backing up DJI action camera footage locally with verification. Concept 01: FRAME VAULT. Original minimal geometric logo combining an open camera viewfinder with two nested archive frames and subtle downward motion in negative space. Mint green #30C9A1 on near-black charcoal, aligned with a dark native Mac app. Large striking symbol above the exact wordmark "ActionVault" in refined contemporary sans-serif. Small monochrome rendition in lower right to demonstrate scalability. Generous negative space, precise optical alignment, sophisticated Swiss graphic design, flat vector-like crisp artwork, no stock shield/checkmark cliché, no DJI or Apple logo, no device mockup, no other text. 3:2 landscape composition, suitable for README product introduction.
```

### 方案 2

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished landscape brand presentation for ActionVault, a macOS action-camera footage backup application. Concept 02: TRAIL ARCHIVE. Original artistic emblem: a single continuous ribbon becomes an abstract mountain A and descends into a protective V shaped archive cradle, evoking adventures safely kept. Warm ivory background, deep forest-green mark with a restrained mint accent. Large centered emblem and exact wordmark "ActionVault" below in carefully spaced humanist sans-serif. Small solid one-color emblem at bottom right. Editorial outdoor design sensibility, timeless, simple silhouette readable at small sizes, flat vector-like artwork. Generous margins. No DJI or Apple logo, no additional text, no photographic scenery, no mockups. 3:2 landscape.
```

### 方案 3

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished landscape brand presentation for ActionVault, native macOS software for verified backup of action-camera memories. Concept 03: MEMORY LENS. A beautifully crafted macOS style rounded-square app icon containing three overlapping translucent frames forming a camera aperture and archive stack, with a clear central circular lens. Mint and teal optical glass, restrained silver edge, subtle dimensional lighting, near-black midnight backdrop. Icon must be original, bold and legible, not overly intricate. Display one large icon centered upper-middle and exact wordmark "ActionVault" beneath in elegant white modern sans-serif. Luxurious product-launch art direction, generous negative space, subtle soft shadow, no other text, no Apple or DJI logos, no screenshot or computer mockup. 3:2 landscape.
```
